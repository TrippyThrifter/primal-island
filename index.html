What you're walking into

This isn't a theme park.

  • 3x growth Reach adulthood fast enough to matter. No multi-day grinds to your first real fight.
  • High AI density The map stays fed. Carnivores hunt, herbivores herd, and the food chain actually holds together.
  • Body drops Carnivores get dropped carcasses when the AI runs thin — the island keeps working even when it's quiet.
  • Low rules Mixpacking, KOS, and cannibalism are part of the ecosystem here, not a bannable offense. Survive accordingly.
  • Free speech chat We moderate hacks and illegal content. We don't moderate your opinions.
  • Dino parking Store a grown dino and come back to it later instead of losing hours of growth to a log-off.
  • Events & quests Scheduled migrations, bounties, and community hunts, with rewards that feed into the auction house.
  • Auction house Grown dinos, skins, and rare mutations, traded player to player.
